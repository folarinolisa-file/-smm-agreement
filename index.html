1. Definitions

  1. "Creative" means one piece of content, such as a single graphic, carousel, or video.
  2. "Post" means one creative published across the platform or platforms included in the Client's plan. The same creative is shared across every included platform. The number of platforms does not multiply the number of creatives produced.

2. Services

The Provider will deliver the plan selected below, as described in the Provider's Rate Card, which forms part of this Agreement. Full plan details are set out in the fields at the end of this page.

3. Term

This Agreement begins on the start date given below and continues monthly until either party ends it. Either party may pause or end the Agreement with at least 2 weeks' notice, given in writing (email or WhatsApp is sufficient).

4. Fees and Payment

  1. Full payment is due before work begins each month, or before a one off job begins under Pay As You Go.

5. What The Client Provides

  1. Brand materials: logo, brand colors, and any existing brand guidelines.
  2. Raw content: product photos, videos, or footage, where the plan does not include full AI content creation.
  3. Login, or admin and collaborator access, to the relevant social media accounts.
  4. One point of contact for approvals and feedback.
  5. Content and approvals at least 2 business days before the scheduled posting date.
  6. Ad account access and budget, where ad campaigns are included.
  7. CRM tool access, where CRM setup is included in the plan.

6. Revisions

Each post includes 1 free round of revisions. Extra changes attract a small additional fee, agreed before the work is done.

7. Ownership

8. Independent Contractor

The Provider is an independent contractor, not an employee of the Client, and is responsible for their own taxes.

9. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential information, such as business plans, login details, and financial information, private, and not to share it with third parties without permission.

10. No Guarantee of Results

The Provider will carry out the Services with reasonable skill and care but does not guarantee specific results, such as follower counts, sales, or engagement, since these depend on factors outside the Provider's control, including platform algorithm changes.

11. Termination

Either party may end this Agreement with 2 weeks' written notice. Fees already paid for work completed are non refundable. Work in progress at the time of termination will be completed or handed over as agreed.
